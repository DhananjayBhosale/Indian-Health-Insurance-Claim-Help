# Messages for missing documents

Short messages the claimant can send to get a missing document. Write them in the claimant's language, keeping document names in English in brackets. Fill each bracket only with facts from the claim; leave it blank rather than guess. The claimant sends them.

Rules:

- One message per provider. Name the patient, the date, and the bill, report, or IP number.
- Ask for a copy of what exists. Never ask anyone to create, back-date, or change a document.
- Leave out the policy number, Aadhaar, and bank details.

**Pharmacy**

> Hello, I bought medicines on [date] for [patient], bill no. [number], ₹[amount]. For a health insurance claim, please share the itemized bill with the patient's name, and the payment receipt.

**Lab or scan centre**

> Hello, [patient] had [test] on [date], bill no. [number]. For a health insurance claim, please share the full signed report (all pages), the bill, and the payment receipt.

**Hospital**

> Hello, [patient] was admitted from [admission date] to [discharge date], IP no. [number]. For the insurance claim, please share: [exact documents, such as the itemized final bill, the discharge summary, or claim form Part B signed and stamped].

**Relative who has the papers**

> Please send me these, one document at a time, as PDFs or clear photos: [list]. For photos, keep the page flat with all four corners visible and no flash. On WhatsApp, send them as Document, not as Photo.
